Edinburgh Accessibility Map

Ever wondered how long it would take to pogo-stick to work? Explore hypothetical journeys around Edinburgh on unconventional transport, from kangaroos to hot air balloons.

Steep hills, cobbles, stairs and kerbs without ramps make everyday journeys hard for people with mobility impairments. Comparing journey times shows how the built environment decides who gets around easily, and who doesn’t.

How to use it
  1. Click the map to set a start and destination. A route from Edinburgh Castle to ECCI is loaded to begin.
  2. Choose how you’ll travel.
  3. Press Get Directions and compare. Red +5 min penalties show the time each obstacle costs.
